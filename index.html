Help

What is SmartSaver?

SmartSaver helps you make better money decisions. Before you buy something, check whether it fits your budget. Track savings goals, log daily spends, use simple calculators.

Everything is on your phone. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Decide — check a purchase

Think of it like asking a friend before you spend. Fill in what you want to buy, the price, your balance, monthly income, and essential bills.

SmartSaver tells you: Yes (safe), Risky (buffer shrinks), or Not yet (not enough).

After checking, save it, save as a template, or share as an image.

Save — savings goals

Set a target (like "New laptop — $500 by March 2026"). Every time you save real money, tap Add money. SmartSaver shows progress and suggests weekly amounts.

Today — log your spends

Each day, log what you spent. It takes 5 seconds. Over a week, SmartSaver shows your trend and compares to last week.

Tools — eight calculators

Savings, budget, debt payoff, discount, percentage, emergency fund, tip, and currency conversion. Works offline.

Streaks

Every day you use the app (log a spend, check a purchase, add to a goal), your streak grows.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use More → Your data → Export to back up before clearing browser data.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once installed as an app.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device

SmartSaver does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on external servers. Everything stays in your browser's local storage.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up. No analytics. What you enter is yours alone.

Your control

Export your data any time. Reset from More. Clearing browser data erases SmartSaver — back up first.

Not financial advice

SmartSaver provides calculations and planning tools, not personalized financial advice.
