Money tips

Rule of thumb

The 50/30/20 rule

Split monthly income into: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and debt payoff.

Safety

Emergency fund first

Aim for 3-6 months of essential expenses in a separate account before investing.

Decision

The 24-hour rule

For any non-essential purchase over a day's income, wait 24 hours. Still want it? Probably a need.

Debt

Pay highest interest first

Focus extra money on the debt with the highest interest rate. It saves the most over time.

Habit

Pay yourself first

Automate savings the day you get paid. What's left is your real spending money.

Tracking

Log small spends too

Small daily spends add up fast. Logging them reveals the leaks in your budget.
